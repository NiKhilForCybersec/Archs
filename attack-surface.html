Attack Surface Management (ASM)

Attack Surface Management continuously discovers, inventories, and monitors external-facing assets. ASM finds the forgotten servers, shadow IT, and exposed services that attackers find first.

The Visibility Problem

Why Traditional Asset Management Fails

Shadow IT: Departments spin up cloud resources without IT knowledge.

M&A: Acquisitions bring unknown infrastructure.

Forgotten Assets: Test servers, old campaigns, decomissioned-but-running systems.

Subsidiaries: Different business units, different security postures.

Cloud Sprawl: Multi-cloud, multi-account complexity.

ASM Best Practices

ASM Guidelines

1. Start with Known Seeds: Provide all known domains, IPs, cloud accounts. Better seeds = better discovery.

2. Validate Unknown Assets: ASM will find things. Validate ownership before acting.

3. Prioritize by Risk: Not all exposed assets are equal. Critical vulns on internet-facing = priority.

4. Integrate with Vuln Management: ASM finds assets, vuln management assesses them. Connect the workflows.

5. Monitor for Changes: New asset = investigation. Something changed = why?

6. Include Subsidiaries: M&A is a major source of unknown exposure. Include all entities.
