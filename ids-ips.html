Intrusion Detection & Prevention Systems

IDS (Intrusion Detection System) monitors network traffic for malicious activity and alerts. IPS (Intrusion Prevention System) goes further by actively blocking detected threats. Modern NGFWs integrate IPS capabilities, but standalone IDS/IPS remains important for network visibility and east-west monitoring.

IDS vs. IPS

IDS (Detection Only)
  • Passive monitoring (copy of traffic)
  • Detects and alerts on threats
  • Does NOT block traffic
  • No latency impact
  • Out-of-band deployment (TAP/SPAN)
  • Requires manual response or SOAR

Use Case: Visibility into network without risk of blocking legitimate traffic. Good for initial deployment or monitoring-only requirements.

IPS (Detection + Prevention)
  • Inline deployment (traffic passes through)
  • Detects AND blocks threats in real-time
  • Actively drops malicious packets
  • Adds latency (microseconds to milliseconds)
  • Single point of failure (needs HA)
  • Automated prevention, no human delay

Use Case: Active protection for critical network segments. Stops attacks before they reach targets.

Detection Methods

Method How It Works Pros Cons
Signature-Based Matches traffic against known attack patterns (like antivirus) Low false positives, fast, proven Can't detect zero-days or novel attacks
Anomaly-Based Learns normal behavior, alerts on deviations Can detect unknown threats Higher false positives, needs tuning
Protocol Analysis Validates protocol conformance (RFC violations) Catches protocol-level attacks, evasion Complex, can break non-compliant apps
Behavioral/Heuristic Identifies malicious behavior patterns Good against polymorphic malware Resource intensive, tuning required

IDS/IPS Best Practices

IDS/IPS Implementation Guidelines

1. Start in Detection Mode: Deploy IPS in IDS mode first. Tune rules, baseline false positives before enabling blocking.

2. Keep Signatures Updated: New vulnerabilities daily. Automated signature updates essential. Test before deploying in blocking mode.

3. SSL/TLS Inspection: 90%+ of traffic is encrypted. Without decryption, IDS/IPS is blind. Deploy SSL inspection where legally permissible.

4. Monitor East-West Traffic: North-south (perimeter) isn't enough. Deploy IDS sensors for internal traffic to catch lateral movement.

5. Integrate with SIEM/SOAR: Alerts are useless without action. Automate enrichment, correlation, and response.

6. High Availability for IPS: Inline IPS is a chokepoint. Deploy in HA pairs with fail-open or fail-close based on risk tolerance.
