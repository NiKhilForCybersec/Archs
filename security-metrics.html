Security Metrics & Reporting

What gets measured gets managed. Security metrics translate security activities into business language, demonstrate program value, identify gaps, and enable data-driven decisions. The right metrics drive the right behaviors.

Metric Categories

Detection Metrics

MTTD, detection coverage, alert volume, false positive rate. Measures how well we find threats.

Response Metrics

MTTR, incident count, containment time. Measures how fast we respond to threats.

Vulnerability Metrics

Patch compliance, mean time to patch, open critical vulns. Measures attack surface reduction.

Compliance Metrics

Control coverage, audit findings, policy exceptions. Measures regulatory adherence.

Risk Metrics

Risk score trends, risk acceptance count, third-party risk. Measures overall risk posture.

Awareness Metrics

Phishing click rate, training completion, report rate. Measures human layer effectiveness.

Trend Analysis

Why Trends Matter More Than Snapshots

A single metric tells you where you are. Trends tell you where you're going.

Ask: Are we getting better over time? Is our investment paying off? Where should we focus next?

Track metrics over 6-12 months minimum. Month-over-month variance is noise; quarter-over-quarter is signal.

Metrics Best Practices

Security Metrics Guidelines

1. Measure What Matters: 5-10 key metrics beat 100 vanity metrics. Focus on outcomes, not activities.

2. Automate Collection: Manual metrics are inaccurate and unsustainable. Pull from tools via API.

3. Provide Context: Numbers without context are meaningless. Include targets, trends, benchmarks.

4. Different Audiences: Board wants risk and business impact. SOC wants operational details. Tailor accordingly.

5. Drive Action: Every metric should have an owner and response plan when thresholds are breached.

6. Review Regularly: Metrics go stale. Annual review of what you measure and why.
