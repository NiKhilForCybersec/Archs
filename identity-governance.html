Identity Governance & Administration

Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) ensures the right people have the right access to the right resources for the right reasons. It encompasses identity lifecycle management, access certification, entitlement management, and separation of duties enforcement.

The Access Accumulation Problem

Without Identity Governance
  • Access granted on request, rarely removed
  • Role changes = keep old access + add new
  • No visibility into who has access to what
  • Terminated employees retain access for weeks
  • Rubber-stamp approvals without review
  • Audit findings: excessive privileges everywhere

Risk: Employee moves from Finance to Marketing, keeps SAP Finance access for 3 years. Never reviewed, never revoked.

With Identity Governance
  • Access tied to roles (RBAC/ABAC)
  • Role changes trigger access recertification
  • Complete visibility: who, what, when, why
  • Automated deprovisioning on termination
  • Periodic access reviews with accountability
  • Continuous compliance and audit-ready

Benefit: Employee moves to Marketing → HR update triggers automatic removal of Finance access, addition of Marketing access. Quarterly review catches any outliers.

IGA Core Components

Identity Lifecycle: Joiner-Mover-Leaver

Employee Lifecycle Example
J
JOINER: New Employee Onboarding
HR creates employee record, triggers identity provisioning
Joiner Workflow
Trigger: HR creates John Smith in Workday
  Department: Finance
  Job Title: Financial Analyst
  Manager: Jane Doe
  Start Date: 2024-02-01

IGA Actions (Automated):
  1. Create AD account: [email protected]
  2. Create Entra ID account (synced)
  3. Assign birthright access:
     - Microsoft 365 E5 license
     - SharePoint: Finance Team site
     - Teams: Finance channel
  4. Assign role-based access:
     - Role: "Financial Analyst"
     - Entitlements: SAP Finance (read), Concur, Workday ESS
  5. Send welcome email with credentials
  6. Create ServiceNow ticket for laptop

Time to Access: < 1 hour from HR entry
M
MOVER: Role Change / Transfer
Employee transfers departments, access must adjust
Mover Workflow
Trigger: HR updates John Smith in Workday
  Old Department: Finance
  New Department: Marketing
  New Title: Marketing Operations Manager
  New Manager: Bob Wilson
  Effective: 2024-06-01

IGA Actions (Automated):
  1. Detect role change event
  2. Calculate access delta:
     REMOVE (old role):
       - SAP Finance access
       - Finance SharePoint site
       - Finance Teams channel
     ADD (new role):
       - Marketo Marketing Automation
       - Marketing SharePoint site
       - Marketing Teams channel
  3. Manager notification:
     "John Smith transferring. Review access."
  4. Grace period: 14 days to complete transition
  5. Automatic removal after grace period

Result: No access accumulation, clean transition
L
LEAVER: Termination / Offboarding
Employee leaves company, all access must be revoked
Leaver Workflow
Trigger: HR terminates John Smith in Workday
  Termination Type: Voluntary resignation
  Last Day: 2024-12-15
  
IGA Actions (Automated - Immediate):
  1. Disable AD account (prevents login)
  2. Revoke all Entra ID sessions
  3. Disable MFA tokens
  4. Block email access
  5. Remove from all groups
  6. Revoke VPN certificates

IGA Actions (Scheduled - 30 days):
  7. Convert mailbox to shared (for manager)
  8. Transfer OneDrive ownership
  9. Archive Teams chats
  10. Delete AD account
  11. Delete Entra ID account

Audit Log:
  All actions timestamped, linked to HR termination
  Evidence retained for 7 years (compliance)

Result: Zero access within 15 minutes of HR action

IGA Best Practices

Identity Governance Success Factors

1. Start with HR as Source of Truth: All identity lifecycle events must flow from HR. If HR doesn't know about it, IGA can't govern it.

2. Implement Role-Based Access: Define roles with appropriate entitlements. Reduces individual access requests by 70%+.

3. Make Reviews Meaningful: Provide reviewers with context: last used date, risk score, peer comparison. Prevents rubber-stamping.

4. Automate Deprovisioning: Leaver process must be automated and immediate. Manual processes leave gaps.

5. Enforce Separation of Duties: Define SoD policies upfront. Block or alert on toxic combinations (e.g., create vendor + approve payment).

6. Use Risk-Based Reviews: High-risk access reviewed more frequently. Not everything needs quarterly review.
